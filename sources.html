Important distinctions

Origins: historical descriptions and genetic models do not establish a single agreed starting date. Milestones: 1977 is the last natural case, 1978 a laboratory-associated outbreak, 1979 certification, and 1980 the formal declaration. Vaccine risks differ by product. The broad historical mortality figure must not be applied to every disease form.
